ANALYST NOTES

Read the measures in context.

  • Revenue is units × unit price × (1 − discount); gross profit subtracts recorded unit cost.
  • Gross margin is calculated from this simulated dataset; it is not a forecast or industry benchmark.
  • The generator intentionally creates repeat purchases and seasonal price effects for practice; patterns are designed, not discovered in real customer data.
