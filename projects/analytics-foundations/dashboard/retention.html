ANALYST NOTES

What cohort analysis helps answer

  • Do newer acquisition cohorts behave differently from earlier cohorts?
  • At which elapsed month does the observed return rate change most?
  • Which cohort comparisons are fair given different follow-up windows?

These are questions for investigation, not claims about a real retailer. Because the generator creates repeat-order patterns by design, findings must be interpreted as a method demonstration.
